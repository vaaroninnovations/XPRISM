{% if IS_TRIAL %}2{% else %}1{% endif %}. Why this lab exists: the 3Rs

These simulations follow the principle of the 3Rs for the humane use of animals in science (Russell & Burch, 1959):

Replacement
Use methods that avoid animals altogether, such as computer simulations, in-vitro methods and models, wherever they achieve the learning objective.
Reduction
Where animals are unavoidable, use the fewest animals needed for a valid result, through good design and statistics.
Refinement
Modify procedures, housing and care to minimise pain, distress and lasting harm, and to improve welfare.

{% if IS_TRIAL %}3{% else %}2{% endif %}. Regulatory and professional framework in India

  • Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960 and the Breeding of and Experiments on Animals (Control and Supervision) Rules, 1998 (amended 2001 and 2006) govern all experiments on animals.
  • CCSEA: the Committee for Control and Supervision of Experiments on Animals (known as CPCSEA until its renaming in January 2023) is the statutory committee under the Act. Institutions using animals must be registered with CCSEA, constitute an Institutional Animal Ethics Committee (IAEC), and obtain approval before any experiment. CCSEA's duty is to ensure animals are not subjected to unnecessary pain or suffering.
  • PCI: the Pharmacy Council of India has amended its Education Regulations so that, wherever animal experiments are prescribed in the pharmacy curriculum, the required knowledge and skills are to be imparted using computer-assisted modules. This follows the 2012 Ministry of Environment & Forests guidelines recommending alternatives to dissection and animal experimentation in teaching.
  • PETA India: People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals (India) is an animal-protection organisation, not a regulator. It campaigned for these changes and promotes non-animal teaching methods, including simulation software, in pharmacy and life-science education.

{% if IS_TRIAL %}4{% else %}3{% endif %}. My undertaking

  • I will use these simulations as teaching aids that replace the use of live animals in routine practical training.
  • I understand that simulated results are generated by models for learning. They are not real data and must not be used for treating humans or animals, or for research or regulatory decisions.
  • If I ever take part in real animal work, I will do so only in a CCSEA-registered establishment, with IAEC approval, proper training and supervision, and with full respect for the 3Rs.
  • I will not use any material from this lab to promote cruelty to animals.
