The 3Rs

Better Science. Fewer Animals. More Responsible Learning.

R1

Replacement

Use interactive computer simulations as alternatives to live-animal practicals in pharmacology education.

R2

Reduction

Allow repeated digital practice and better preparation, supporting efforts to reduce unnecessary animal use.

R3

Refinement

Teach humane procedures, animal welfare, careful experimental planning and responsible research practice.
