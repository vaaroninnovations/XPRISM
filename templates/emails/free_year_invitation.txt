ABOUT {{ site_name }}

{{ site_name }} ({{ site_full_name }}) offers computer-assisted simulation learning with LMS, as per the PCI syllabus and CCSEA guidelines, along with 3R recommendations. It gives students realistic, interactive alternatives to animal experiments, so that practical skills can be learned without harming animals.

- Replacement: interactive simulations of the experimental pharmacology practicals in the B.Pharm curriculum.
- Reduction: students can repeat a practical as often as they need, so any animal work that is still required begins only after the method has been mastered.
- Refinement: each module teaches correct handling, dosing, humane end-points and the observations that matter.
- Learning management: batches, class codes, assignments, automatic marking, exam mode, grading and feedback, Excel marks sheets and an audit log.
