What XPRISM offers

  • Replacement: interactive simulations of the experimental pharmacology practicals in the B.Pharm curriculum, including behavioural studies, isolated tissue preparations and bioassays, so that they can be learned without using animals.
  • Reduction: students can repeat a practical as often as they need, so where animal work is still required, it only begins after the method has been mastered.
  • Refinement: each module teaches correct handling, dosing, humane end-points and the observations that matter, so any future animal work causes the least possible distress.
  • Ethics and compliance: every user accepts an ethics agreement based on the 3Rs and on the guidelines of CCSEA and the Pharmacy Council of India.
  • Learning that can be assessed: faculty assign experiments, students submit observation sheets and viva answers, and marks are recorded securely.
