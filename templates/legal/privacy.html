This policy explains what personal data XPRISM, the Experimental Pharmacology Research & Interactive Student Module ("the Lab", "we"), collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Who is responsible for your data

{{ LEGAL.org_name }}, {{ LEGAL.org_address }}, operates the Lab and is the Data Fiduciary for data collected through it. Where your college uses the Lab for its students, your college decides which students and staff are enrolled and how marks are used. For those purposes your college is also a Data Fiduciary, and we process data on its instructions.

3. Why we use it (purposes) and our legal basis

  • To provide the Lab: create your account, show you your assignments, save and score your submissions. Basis: your consent, given at sign-up or first sign-in.
  • Teaching and assessment: let your faculty review your work, give marks and feedback, and export marks for your college's internal assessment. Basis: consent, and your college's legitimate educational purpose as a use for which you voluntarily provided data (DPDP Act, s. 7(a)).
  • Security: protect accounts (e.g. lock after repeated failed sign-ins), investigate misuse, keep an audit trail. Basis: consent and legal obligations on reasonable security safeguards.
  • Free trial: enforce the one-visit-per-module rule and respond to your subscription enquiries. Basis: your consent.
  • Free-year applications: verify your application, contact you about it and, if it is approved, set up your access. Basis: your consent, given on the application form.
  • Ethics agreement: record that you accepted the trial rules and the ethical-use-of-animals undertaking (version and time). Basis: consent.
  • Service e-mails: account messages such as password reset and verification, and messages about your free-year application.
  • Invitations: our administrators may e-mail a college contact one invitation to apply for the free year. Every invitation has an opt-out link, and we keep the address only to record that invitation and honour any opt-out. We do not send any other marketing.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, do not show advertising, do not profile you for marketing, and do not use your data to train artificial-intelligence models.

4. Who can see your data

  • You: your own profile, submissions, marks and feedback.
  • Your faculty: data of students in the batches they teach.
  • Lab assistants of your college: enrolment and progress, without the ability to change marks.
  • Your college admin: data of all users of your college, to manage accounts and handle your requests.
  • Platform administrators of {{ LEGAL.org_name }}: only as needed to run, secure and support the service, including managing free-trial accounts and individual subscriptions.
  • Service providers (Data Processors): the hosting provider of our server in {{ LEGAL.hosting_location }} and, if configured, an e-mail delivery service for password resets. They process data only on our instructions under contract.
  • Authorities: only when required by law, court order or a lawful request.

Students of one college cannot see data of another college, and students cannot see each other's submissions or marks.

5. How long we keep it

  • Accounts and academic records: while your college uses the Lab and for {{ DATA_RETENTION_YEARS }} years after your last activity, so that internal-assessment marks can be verified. After that, personal data is erased or de-identified.
  • Audit and security logs: up to 1 year, or longer if needed for an ongoing investigation, and at least as long as the law requires.
  • When an account is deleted on request, your name, roll number, e-mail, submissions and feedback are erased. Your college may keep de-identified marks (not linked to you) for statistics.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  1. Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed. Use My data → Download all my data at any time.
  2. Correction and completion of inaccurate or incomplete data. Ask from My data or ask your faculty.
  3. Erasure of your data when it is no longer needed, or when you withdraw consent, subject to legal retention.
  4. Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by requesting account deletion. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it. Without consent you will not be able to use the Lab for assessed work.
  5. Grievance redressal (section 9) and, if not satisfied, to complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  6. Nominate another person to exercise these rights in the event of your death or incapacity, by writing to the grievance officer.

We aim to respond within 30 days, and in any case within the period prescribed under the DPDP Rules.

7. Children

The Lab is intended for students of degree and diploma pharmacy programmes, who are normally 18 or older. If you are under 18, you may use the Lab only with the verifiable consent of your parent or lawful guardian, obtained through your college. We do not track or monitor children's behaviour, and we do not direct advertising at anyone. If we learn that a child's data was collected without such consent, we will delete it.

9. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Access is restricted by role and college. Accounts lock after repeated failed sign-ins. Key actions are written to an audit log that cannot be edited. The server and backups are access-controlled. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as required by the DPDP Rules.
