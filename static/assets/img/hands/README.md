# Gloved-hand renders

The gloved hand in Exp 01, 04, 09 and 21 is still drawn in code. It is to be replaced by three
photo-style renders, in the same way as the rabbit. Put the three files below in this folder.
They are then cut out, saved as `.webp` and fitted into the benches.

## What every image needs

- One hand only, in a **blue nitrile glove**, with the cuff of a **white lab coat** at the wrist.
- **No animal, syringe or other object** in the picture. The bench draws the animal itself, and
  the fingers are laid over it.
- A **plain, flat, light-grey or white background** with no shadow on it, so the hand can be cut out.
- Soft, even light from the upper left. No hard shadows, no motion blur, nothing cropped.
- At least **1500 px** on the long side, PNG or JPG.
- An image you have the right to use. An AI render you made yourself needs no credit.

## The three poses

| File | Used in | Pose |
|---|---|---|
| `scruff.png` | Exp 01, routes of administration | **Side view from the thumb side.** The forearm comes in from the upper right. Thumb and index finger point down and pinch together, as if lifting a small fold of skin. The other three fingers are loosely curled behind. |
| `tail.png` | Exp 04 Rota-Rod and Exp 09 actophotometer | **Back of the hand, seen straight on.** The wrist is at the top and the fingers point straight down. Thumb and index finger pinch together at the bottom, as if holding a thin cord that hangs down. |
| `hold.png` | Exp 21, paw oedema | **Hand reaching in from the upper right, fingers pointing left.** The fingers are gently curled around the back of something the size of a rat, with the thumb on top. The forearm and sleeve run up to the top right corner. |
