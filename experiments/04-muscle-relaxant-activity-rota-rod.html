MODULE 04 Examination 4: To Study the Muscle Relaxant Activity of a Given Drug Using the Rota-Rod Apparatus
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Evaluation of Skeletal Muscle Relaxant Activity & Motor Coordination Using the Rota-Rod

Assess the effect of centrally acting muscle relaxants and CNS depressants—the benzodiazepine standard Diazepam (4 mg/kg i.p.), the phenothiazine Chlorpromazine (3 mg/kg i.p.) and the barbiturate Phenobarbitone sodium (40 mg/kg i.p.)—on the motor coordination (grip strength and balance) of albino mice placed on a rotating rod. Caffeine (10 mg/kg i.p.) serves as a negative comparator.

1

Principle

A normal mouse keeps its balance on a slowly rotating knurled rod (≈3 cm diameter, 20–25 rpm) indefinitely by walking against the rotation. Drugs that relax skeletal muscle or impair motor coordination reduce grip and balance, so the animal falls off sooner. The time to fall (fall-off time) is the index of muscle relaxant activity.

2

Screening & Design

Only mice that remain on the rod for the full cut-off time (180 s) during the basal trial are accepted. Each mouse acts as its own control: the basal fall-off time is recorded, the drug is injected i.p., and the post-drug fall-off time is recorded 30 min later (n = 6).

3

Calculation

% Decrease in motor coordination = ((Basal − Post-drug) / Basal) × 100. Report Mean ± SEM and compare basal vs post-drug values by Student's paired t-test (df = n − 1).

Requirements

  • Apparatus: Rota-rod (6 compartments, knurled rod 3 cm, variable 5–40 rpm, trip-plate timers)
  • Animals: Albino mice, 20–28 g, either sex (n = 6)
  • Drugs: Diazepam injection (1 mg/mL), Chlorpromazine (0.5 mg/mL), Phenobarbitone sodium (4 mg/mL), Caffeine (1 mg/mL)
  • Others: 1 mL tuberculin syringe, 26G needle, weighing balance, stopwatch

Procedure

  1. Switch ON the rota-rod and set the speed to 20–25 rpm.
  2. Weigh and number the mice; place one mouse in each compartment.
  3. Press START and note the basal fall-off time (cut-off 180 s).
  4. Inject the test drug i.p. (dose volume from body weight).
  5. After 30 min, repeat the trial at the same speed.
  6. Tabulate, calculate % decrease, apply the paired t-test.
