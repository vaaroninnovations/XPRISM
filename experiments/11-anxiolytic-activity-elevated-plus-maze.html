MODULE 11 Examination 11: To Study the Anxiolytic Activity of a Given Drug Using the Elevated Plus Maze in Mice
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Anxiolytic Activity Using the Elevated Plus Maze (EPM)

Evaluate the anxiolytic effect of a test drug in mice on the elevated plus maze. Mice are given vehicle or drug i.p. 30 min before a 5-minute test. Count the number of entries into, and time the time spent in, the open and closed arms. Anxiolytics increase the % open-arm entries and the % time in open arms.

You do every step yourself: lift mice from the stock cage, mark them with picric acid, weigh them and allocate them by drawing lots; calculate the dose, weigh the drug and prepare the solution; inject i.p. and note the time; after 30 min set the light, place each mouse on the centre of the maze, start the 5-minute timer and count its entries and time in the arms from its movements; wipe the maze between animals; then analyse your own data.

1

Principle

Mice naturally avoid open, elevated spaces and prefer enclosed ones. The conflict between curiosity (exploring the open arms) and fear of heights and openness is a model of anxiety. Anxiolytics reduce the fear, so the mouse explores the open arms more.

2

Apparatus

Two open arms (30 × 5 cm) and two closed arms (30 × 5 cm with 15 cm walls) joined by a 5 × 5 cm centre, raised 50 cm above the floor. An arm entry counts when all four paws are inside the arm. Dim light (about 100 lux); clean the maze with 70% ethanol between animals.

3

Calculations

% open-arm entries = open ÷ (open + closed) × 100

% open-arm time = open time ÷ (open + closed time) × 100

Total entries reflect general activity. Compare groups by unpaired t-test (n = 6).

Requirements

  • Swiss albino mice 20–30 g, 6 per group (not fasted)
  • Vehicle: normal saline 10 mL/kg i.p.
  • Diazepam 1 mg/kg; alprazolam 0.25 mg/kg; buspirone 2 mg/kg; caffeine 30 mg/kg (all i.p., 10 mL/kg)
  • Elevated plus maze, timer, tally counters, 70% ethanol, balances, 1 mL syringes with 26 G needles

Procedure

  1. Mark, weigh and randomise 12 mice into 2 groups of 6. Prepare the drug solution; inject vehicle (Group I) or test drug (Group II) i.p. and note the time.
  2. After 30 min, place one mouse on the central platform, facing an open arm; start the 5-minute timer.
  3. Count open and closed arm entries, time in each arm and head-dips over the open-arm edges.
  4. Return the mouse to its cage and wipe the maze with 70% ethanol before the next animal.
  5. Calculate %OE, %OT and total entries; compare the groups.
