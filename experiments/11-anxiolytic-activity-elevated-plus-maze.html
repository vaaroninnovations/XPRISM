MODULE 11 Examination 11: To Study the Anxiolytic Activity of a Given Drug Using the Elevated Plus Maze in Mice
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Anxiolytic Activity Using the Elevated Plus Maze (EPM)

Evaluate the anxiolytic effect of a test drug in mice on the elevated plus maze. Mice are given vehicle or drug i.p. 30 min before a 5-minute test. Record the number of entries into, and the time spent in, the open and closed arms. Anxiolytics increase the % open-arm entries and the % time in open arms.

1

Principle

Mice naturally avoid open, elevated spaces and prefer enclosed ones. The conflict between curiosity (exploring the open arms) and fear of heights and openness is a model of anxiety. Anxiolytics reduce the fear, so the mouse explores the open arms more.

2

Apparatus

Two open arms (30 × 5 cm) and two closed arms (30 × 5 × 15 cm walls) joined by a 5 × 5 cm centre, raised 50 cm above the floor. An arm entry counts when all four paws are inside the arm. Clean the maze with 70% ethanol between animals.

3

Calculations

% open-arm entries = open ÷ (open + closed) × 100. % open-arm time = open time ÷ (open + closed time) × 100. Total entries reflect general activity. Compare groups by unpaired t-test (n = 6).

Procedure

  1. Inject vehicle (Group I) or test drug (Group II) i.p. and wait 30 min.
  2. Place one mouse on the central platform, facing an open arm; start the 5-minute timer.
  3. Record open/closed arm entries, time in each arm and head-dips over the open-arm edges.
  4. Return the mouse to its cage and wipe the maze with 70% ethanol before the next animal.
