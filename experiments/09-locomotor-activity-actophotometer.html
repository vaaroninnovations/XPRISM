MODULE 09 Examination 9: To Study the Locomotor Activity of Given Drug Using the Actophotometer
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Study of CNS Depressant and Stimulant Activity Using the Actophotometer

Evaluate the effect of CNS-active drugs—the sedative-anxiolytic standard Diazepam (2 mg/kg p.o.), the neuroleptic Chlorpromazine (2 mg/kg p.o.) and the CNS stimulants Caffeine (10 mg/kg p.o.) and Amphetamine (2.5 mg/kg p.o.)—on the spontaneous locomotor activity and exploratory rearing of albino mice (Mus musculus) using a digital actophotometer. You catch, weigh, number and randomise the mice, prepare the drug, calculate and give every dose by oral gavage, run each 3-minute session yourself and count the rearings by eye with a tally counter.

1

Photocell Principle

The actophotometer is a square chamber with rows of infrared transmitters on two walls and photo-electric receivers opposite them. As the animal moves across the arena it breaks the beams; each interruption is registered as one count on the digital counter.

2

Behavioural Endpoints

Record the Locomotor score (beam interruptions / 3 min) and the Rearing count (standing on hind limbs), which you count yourself. Rearing reflects exploratory drive; a fall indicates sedation or motor impairment, a rise indicates stimulation.

3

Experimental Design

Two randomised groups (n = 6 each): Group I: Control (vehicle 10 mL/kg p.o.) and Group II: Drug-treated. Test 30–60 min after dosing. Calculate Mean ± SEM, % reduction or stimulation, and compare the groups by the unpaired t-test (df = 10).

Physiological Baseline & Experimental Norms (Albino Mice: 20–25 g)

Test Duration: 3 minutes / animal
Control Count: ~150–165 counts / 3 min
Control Rearings: 3–5 rearings / 3 min
Diazepam Effect: ≈ 50–65 % decrease in counts

Requirements

  • Apparatus: Digital actophotometer (30 × 30 cm perspex arena, 6 × 6 IR beams, counter and session timer)
  • Animals: Swiss albino mice, male, 20–25 g (12 used, 6 per group)
  • Drugs (25 mL, dosed at 10 mL/kg): Diazepam 0.2 mg/mL suspension in 0.5 % CMC; Chlorpromazine HCl 0.2 mg/mL, Caffeine citrate 1 mg/mL, Amphetamine sulfate 0.25 mg/mL in distilled water
  • Others: top-pan and analytical balances, mortar & pestle, 25 mL volumetric flask, 1 mL syringe with 20G ball-tipped feeding needle, picric acid, random number table, 70 % ethanol, hand tally counter

Procedure

  1. Weigh each mouse in a tared beaker, number it with picric acid and allocate it to Group I or II using the random number table.
  2. Prepare the drug; calculate 10 mL/kg for each mouse.
  3. Give Group I the vehicle and Group II the drug by oral gavage, 3–4 min apart; note each time.
  4. Switch on the actophotometer and set the session to 3 min.
  5. 30 min after its dose, wipe the arena with 70 % ethanol, reset the counter, place the mouse in the centre and press START. Count rearings with the tally counter.
  6. Record counts and rearings; calculate mean ± SEM, % change and the unpaired t-test.
Ethics (CCSEA, 3Rs): restrain gently by the scruff for gavage, never force the feeding needle against resistance, keep oral volumes ≤ 10–20 mL/kg and monitor every animal after dosing. Test in a quiet, dimly lit room. This virtual practical replaces animal use for training.
