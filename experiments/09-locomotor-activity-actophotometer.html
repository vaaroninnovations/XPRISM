MODULE 09 Examination 9: To Study the Locomotor Activity of Given Drug Using the Actophotometer
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Study of CNS Depressant and Stimulant Activity Using the Actophotometer

Evaluate the effect of Central Nervous System (CNS) active drugs—including the sedative-anxiolytic standard Diazepam (1.0 mg/kg p.o.), neuroleptic Chlorpromazine (2.0 mg/kg p.o.), and CNS stimulants like Caffeine (10 mg/kg p.o.)—on the spontaneous locomotor activity and exploratory rearing behavior of albino mice (Mus musculus) using an automated digital Actophotometer.

1

Photocell Principle

The actophotometer consists of a square chamber surrounded by opposing pairs of infrared light transmitters and photo-electric receivers. As the animal ambulates across the arena, it breaks the light beams; each beam interruption is registered as one discrete digital count.

2

Behavioral Endpoints

Record both the Locomotor Index (beam interruptions / 3 min) and the Rearing Count (standing on hind limbs). High rearings reflect exploratory drive and curiosity; reduction indicates sedation, motor incoordination, or anxiolysis.

3

Experimental Design

Animals are divided into two matched groups (n = 6 per group): Group I: Control (Vehicle / Distilled Water) and Group II: Drug-Treated. Calculate Mean ± SEM and percentage (%) reduction or stimulation in motility.
