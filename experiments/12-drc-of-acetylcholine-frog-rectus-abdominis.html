MODULE 12 Examination 12: To Record the Dose–Response Curve of Acetylcholine Using the Isolated Frog Rectus Abdominis Muscle
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Graded Dose–Response Curve of Acetylcholine on the Isolated Frog Rectus Abdominis

You will carry out the whole practical yourself, exactly as at the bench: prepare the Frog Ringer and the acetylcholine (ACh) dilutions, dissect out the rectus abdominis, assemble the organ bath, lever and kymograph, mount and equilibrate the tissue, give each dose with a tuberculin syringe, time the contact, wash, rest, measure every contraction and analyse your own readings. Nothing is done for you: mistakes in technique show up in your record just as they would in the laboratory.

1

Principle

The frog rectus abdominis contains multiply-innervated tonic fibres with nicotinic NM receptors along the whole fibre. ACh produces a slow, sustained contracture whose height is graded with the dose.

2

Time cycle

90 s contact → wash 2–3 times → rest 5 min → next dose. Re-dosing too early desensitises the receptors; poor washing leaves ACh behind and the baseline does not return.

3

Analysis

Response vs dose is a rectangular hyperbola; % response vs log dose is a sigmoid. The EC50 is read from the log-DRC. Physostigmine shifts it left; d-tubocurarine shifts it right in parallel.

Frog Ringer solution (per litre)

Sodium chloride (NaCl)6.5 g
Potassium chloride (KCl)0.14 g
Calcium chloride, anhydrous (CaCl2)0.12 g
Sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3)0.20 g
Sodium dihydrogen phosphate (NaH2PO4)0.01 g
Glucose2.0 g

You will prepare 2 litres. Dissolve everything except CaCl2 in about 1.8 L of distilled water, add CaCl2 last (otherwise calcium carbonate/phosphate precipitates), make up to the 2000 mL mark and mix.

Procedure

  1. Prepare 2 L Frog Ringer. From ACh 1 mg/mL make 10 mL each of 100 µg/mL and 10 µg/mL in Ringer (C1V1 = C2V2).
  2. Confirm the frog is pithed (no corneal or withdrawal reflex), pin it supine, open the skin in the midline and reflect it.
  3. Tie threads at the pubic and sternal ends of one belly of the rectus, cut it free and place it in Ringer.
  4. Fill the reservoir and the 20 mL bath, aerate gently, fix smoked paper on the kymograph and choose the slowest speed.
  5. Tie the tissue to the holder, lower it into the bath, tie the upper thread to the frontal lever, load with 1 g, level the lever and let the writing point touch the paper lightly.
  6. Equilibrate for 30 min, washing every 10 min.
  7. Give ACh in doubling doses (0.5, 1, 2, 4 … µg): 90 s contact, then wash 2–3 times and rest 5 min. Continue until two doses give the same height.
  8. Measure every response from the baseline with the ruler, write dose and height in your notebook, then complete the table, plot the graphs and read the EC50.
