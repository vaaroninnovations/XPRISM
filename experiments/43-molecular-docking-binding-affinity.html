MODULE 43 Examination 43: In Silico Drug Discovery: Molecular Docking and Binding Affinity
OBJECTIVE

Molecular Docking

Docking predicts how a small molecule might bind to a protein. You will prepare a docking run, validate it, and interpret results for a fictional target. This is a teaching exercise using supplied results; it does not run a real docking engine.

Workflow

Prepare the protein, prepare the ligand, define the search box around the binding site, run the docking, then examine the poses and scores.

Reading a score

A more negative binding energy predicts stronger binding. An estimated Ki follows from the energy:

Ki = e^(ΔG ÷ RT), RT = 0.593 kcal/mol at 298 K

Validation

Re-dock the known ligand from the crystal structure. An RMSD of 2 Å or less from the crystal pose is the usual sign that the method can reproduce the binding mode.
