MODULE 21 Examination 21: To Study the Anti-inflammatory Activity of a Given Drug by Carrageenan-Induced Paw Oedema in Rats
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Acute Anti-inflammatory Activity: Carrageenan-Induced Rat Paw Oedema (Winter et al., 1962)

Inject 0.1 mL of 1% w/v carrageenan under the plantar skin of the right hind paw and measure the paw volume with a plethysmometer before and 1, 2, 3 and 4 h afterwards. The test drug is given orally 1 h before carrageenan. An anti-inflammatory drug reduces the oedema compared with the vehicle group.

1

Principle: a biphasic oedema

Early phase (0–1.5 h): histamine, 5-HT and bradykinin. Late phase (2.5–4 h): prostaglandins from inducible COX-2, with neutrophil infiltration. NSAIDs mainly inhibit the late phase; corticosteroids inhibit both; antihistamine/anti-5-HT drugs inhibit only the early phase.

2

Plethysmometer technique

The paw is marked with ink at the lateral malleolus. Each time, dip it into the measuring cell exactly up to the mark. The displaced fluid is read in mL; press the HOLD pedal to capture the reading. Zero the instrument before use.

3

Calculation

Oedema = Vt − V0 (mL).
% Inhibition = (1 − Etest ÷ Econtrol) × 100, using the mean oedema at each time point. Compare the groups by unpaired t-test (n = 6).

Requirements

  • Digital plethysmometer (water cell with wetting agent), HOLD foot pedal
  • Albino rats, 150–200 g, fasted overnight with water ad libitum
  • Carrageenan 1% w/v in normal saline (0.1 mL per paw), 26 G needle
  • Indomethacin 10 mg/kg p.o., Diclofenac 10 mg/kg p.o., Aspirin 100 mg/kg p.o., Paracetamol 100 mg/kg p.o., Dexamethasone 1 mg/kg s.c., Cyproheptadine 10 mg/kg p.o.

Procedure

  1. Switch on the plethysmometer and press ZERO with the cell empty of any paw.
  2. Measure the basal volume (V0) of the right hind paw of each rat.
  3. Give vehicle (Group I) or the test drug (Group II) orally.
  4. After 1 h inject 0.1 mL of 1% carrageenan sub-plantar into the right hind paw.
  5. Measure the paw volume at 1, 2, 3 and 4 h; calculate oedema and % inhibition.
