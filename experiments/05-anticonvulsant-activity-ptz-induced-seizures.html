MODULE 05 Examination 5: To Study the Anticonvulsant Activity of a Given Drug Against Pentylenetetrazole (PTZ)-Induced Seizures in Mice
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Anticonvulsant Screening by the Pentylenetetrazole (Chemoconvulsant) Test

Pre-treat albino mice with a test drug, challenge them 30 min later with pentylenetetrazole (PTZ) 80 mg/kg i.p., and observe for 30 min. Record the latency to myoclonic jerks and to generalized clonic seizures, the occurrence of tonic hind-limb extension, and mortality. Compare with a vehicle control group to find the % protection.

1

Principle

PTZ is a non-competitive GABA-A receptor antagonist (picrotoxin site). By blocking GABAergic inhibition it produces myoclonic and clonic seizures. The test predicts efficacy against absence and myoclonic epilepsy, whereas the MES test (Exp #3) predicts efficacy against generalized tonic-clonic seizures.

2

Seizure Stages (modified Racine)

0 normal • 1 ear/facial twitching • 2 myoclonic body jerks • 3 forelimb clonus (sitting) • 4 generalized clonic seizure with loss of righting • 5 tonic hind-limb extension, often followed by death.

3

Protection

A mouse is protected if it shows no generalized clonic seizure during the 30-min observation. Animals without an event are given the cut-off latency (1800 s). Compare latencies by Student's t-test and % protection by Fisher's exact test.

Requirements

  • Animals: Albino mice, 20–28 g, two groups of 6
  • Convulsant: Pentylenetetrazole 8 mg/mL in saline (80 mg/kg = 10 mL/kg i.p.)
  • Drugs: Diazepam 4 mg/kg, Ethosuximide 150 mg/kg, Phenobarbitone 30 mg/kg, Phenytoin 25 mg/kg, Sodium valproate 200 mg/kg (all i.p.)
  • Others: 6-compartment perspex observation box, stopwatch, 1 mL syringes, 26G needles

Procedure

  1. Weigh and number the mice; divide them into Group I (vehicle) and Group II (test drug).
  2. Inject vehicle or test drug i.p. and wait 30 min.
  3. Inject PTZ 80 mg/kg i.p. and place each mouse in its own compartment.
  4. Start the stopwatch; note the onset of each seizure stage for 30 min.
  5. Record tonic extension and death; calculate % protection.
