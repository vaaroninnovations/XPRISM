MODULE 27 Examination 27: To Determine the Cytotoxicity and IC50 of an Anticancer Drug by the MTT Assay
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

MTT Assay: Cytotoxicity and IC50 of an Anticancer Drug

Measure how a drug reduces the viability of cultured cancer cells. Mitochondrial and cytosolic NAD(P)H-dependent enzymes in metabolically active cells reduce the yellow tetrazolium salt MTT to insoluble purple formazan. Once dissolved, the colour is read at 570 nm, and absorbance is proportional to the number of viable cells. Construct a log concentration-response curve and estimate the IC50.

Every stage feeds the next. Poor aseptic technique, the wrong seeding density, edge effects or lost formazan crystals all show up in your blanks, replicates and curve. Cell lines are simulated, and no animal is used.

1

Prepare the cells

Select a healthy, 70–90% confluent, uncontaminated flask. Trypsinise, count with trypan blue and dilute.
Cells/mL = mean count × 2 × 104
Vcells = Vtotal × target ÷ count

2

Seed the 96-well plate

Seed 1 × 104 cells in 100 µL per well. Fill the outer wells with PBS, because they evaporate faster (the edge effect). Leave one column without cells as the blank. Allow 24 h at 37 °C, 5% CO2 for attachment.

3

Treat with the drug

Prepare a 1:3 serial dilution from the stock.
C1V1 = C2V2
The vehicle control must contain the same % DMSO as the highest drug concentration. Incubate for 48 h.

4

Add MTT

Add 10 µL of 5 mg/mL MTT in PBS (filter-sterilised, protected from light) to each well. Incubate for 3–4 h. Viable cells form purple needle-like formazan crystals.

5

Dissolve the formazan

Remove the medium gently without disturbing the crystals. Add 100 µL of DMSO to each well and shake for 15 min, protected from light, until the colour is uniform.

6

Read and calculate

Read at 570 nm with a 630 nm reference.
% viability = (ODtest − ODblank) ÷ (ODvehicle − ODblank) × 100
IC50 is the concentration giving 50% viability on the log curve.

Requirements

  • Cell line and drug pair: MCF-7 with doxorubicin, HeLa with cisplatin, or HT-29 with 5-fluorouracil
  • Complete medium (10% FBS), PBS, 0.25% trypsin-EDTA, trypan blue, 70% ethanol
  • MTT, DMSO, 0.22 µm syringe filter, aluminium foil
  • Biosafety cabinet, CO2 incubator, inverted microscope, haemocytometer, centrifuge
  • Flat-bottom 96-well plate, multichannel pipette, plate shaker, microplate reader

Plate layout (rows B–G, n = 6 per column)

  • Outer wells: PBS only
  • Column 2: blank (medium and MTT, no cells)
  • Column 3: vehicle control (cells with matching DMSO)
  • Columns 4–10: seven drug concentrations, highest to lowest
  • Column 11: untreated cells (checks that the vehicle itself is not toxic)

MTT measures metabolic activity, not cell number directly. Drugs that alter metabolism can give misleading results, so research work confirms findings with a second method.
