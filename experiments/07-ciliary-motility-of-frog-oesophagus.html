MODULE 07 Examination 07: To Study the Effect of Acetylcholine and Atropine on the Ciliary Motility of Frog Oesophagus
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Effect of Drugs on Ciliary Motility of the Frog Oesophagus

The ciliated epithelium of the frog's buccal cavity and oesophagus carries particles towards the stomach. Place a poppy seed at the oral end of the opened oesophagus and measure the time it takes to travel a fixed distance (20 mm) in frog Ringer (control), after acetylcholine, after physostigmine, after atropine, and after acetylcholine in the presence of atropine.

You do every step yourself: weigh the salts and make Frog Ringer, dilute the ACh ampoule (C1V1 = C2V2) and make the physostigmine and atropine solutions; pin the (staff-pithed) frog, remove the lower jaw, open the oesophagus, pin it mucosa upwards and mark 20 mm; apply each drug with a dropper, wait the contact time, place the seed with forceps, run the stopwatch yourself, rinse and wait for recovery. You type every time into the notebook and calculate the means, velocities and % changes. Mistakes (dry mucosa, wrong dilution, short contact, poor washing, wrong order, unrinsed dropper) show up in your data.

1

Principle

Ciliary beating and mucus secretion in the frog oesophagus are increased by muscarinic (M) stimulation. Faster beating moves the seed faster, so the travel time falls.

2

Expected

ACh: time ↓ (motility ↑). Physostigmine: time ↓ (more endogenous ACh). Atropine: time ↑ slightly and it blocks the ACh effect.

3

Measure

Start the stop-watch when the seed crosses the start mark and stop it at the finish mark. Velocity (mm/s) = 20 ÷ time. Take 3 readings per treatment.

Requirements

  • Frog (Hoplobatrachus tigerinus, formerly Rana tigrina), pithed by trained staff (brain and spinal cord destroyed) as approved by the IAEC / CCSEA
  • Dissection board, pins, scissors, forceps, frog Ringer solution, dropper
  • Poppy seeds, stop-watch, scale (marks 20 mm apart)
  • Acetylcholine 100 µg/mL, physostigmine 1 mg/mL, atropine sulphate 1 mg/mL

Procedure

  1. Cut the lower jaw and open the oesophagus to the stomach; pin it on the board, mucosa upwards.
  2. Keep the tissue moist with Ringer. Mark start and finish points 20 mm apart.
  3. Place a poppy seed at the oral end and time its travel (3 trials). This is the control.
  4. Add 2–3 drops of ACh, wait 5 min and take 3 readings. Wash with Ringer and allow 10 min.
  5. Repeat with physostigmine; then atropine; then ACh in the presence of atropine.
