MODULE 10 Examination 10: To Study Haloperidol-Induced Catalepsy (Bar Test) and Amphetamine-Induced Stereotypy in Rats, and their Modification by Drugs
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Dopamine-Related Motor Behaviour: Catalepsy and Stereotypy

Part A: haloperidol (1 mg/kg i.p.) blocks striatal D2 receptors and makes rats cataleptic: they keep an imposed posture. Measure with a stopwatch how long each rat keeps its forepaws on a horizontal bar and test whether a drug reverses the catalepsy.

Part B: amphetamine (5 mg/kg i.p.) releases striatal dopamine and causes stereotyped sniffing, licking and gnawing. Score the behaviour and test whether a drug antagonises it.

You do every step yourself: lift rats from the stock cage, mark them with picric acid, weigh them and allocate them by drawing lots; calculate the doses, weigh the drugs and make up the solutions; give the inducer and then the vehicle or test drug i.p. and note the times; take a basal reading and then readings at 30, 60, 90 and 120 min with the stopwatch or the scoring scale; record everything in your notebook and do the statistics.

A

Bar test for catalepsy

Gently place both forepaws of the rat on a steel bar 9 cm above the base, hind paws on the base. Start the stopwatch and stop it when the rat removes both forepaws (descent latency). Use a cut-off of 180 s: lift the rat off and record 180 s. Test before the drugs (basal) and at 30, 60, 90 and 120 min.

B

Stereotypy scoring (0–4)

Observe each rat in a perspex cage for at least 30 s and score: 0 normal activity; 1 discontinuous sniffing with exploration; 2 continuous sniffing, periodic exploration; 3 continuous sniffing with intermittent licking or gnawing; 4 continuous licking or gnawing, no exploration.

Requirements

  • Bar test apparatus (steel bar 0.9 cm diameter, 9 cm high), stopwatch
  • Perspex observation cage, albino rats 150–200 g, 12 per part (6 per group)
  • Haloperidol 1 mg/kg i.p.; scopolamine 1 mg/kg; trihexyphenidyl 5 mg/kg; amantadine 40 mg/kg; diazepam 2 mg/kg (Part A test drugs)
  • d-Amphetamine 5 mg/kg i.p.; haloperidol 1 mg/kg; chlorpromazine 3 mg/kg; propranolol 10 mg/kg; diazepam 2 mg/kg (Part B test drugs)
  • All injections 5 mL/kg i.p.; analytical balance, animal balance, 2 mL syringes

Calculation

  • Part A: mean descent latency (s) ± SEM at each time; % reversal = (1 − Test ÷ Control) × 100; unpaired t-test.
  • Part B: scores are ordinal: report the median (and mean ± SEM); % inhibition of the mean score; Mann–Whitney U test.
  • Group I receives the inducer + vehicle, Group II the inducer + test drug (n = 6). Time zero = the inducer injection.
