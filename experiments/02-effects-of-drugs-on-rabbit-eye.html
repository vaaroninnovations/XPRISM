MODULE 02 Examination 2: Effects of Drugs on Rabbit's Eye
EXPERIMENT OBJECTIVE

Effects of Autonomic & Anesthetic Drugs on the Rabbit's Eye

Test the prescribed pharmacological agents, including an Unknown Mystery Drug, record baseline and post-drug observations in the standardized table, analyze pupil diameter, light reflex, corneal reflex, and intraocular pressure, and deduce the identity of the unknown drug.

1

Drug Instillation

Instill test compounds into the Right Eye (Treated). The Left Eye (Control) remains under Normal Saline for continuous physiological comparison.

2

Scale & Reflexes

Activate the Pupil Scale tool: click and drag across the pupil to automatically compute millimeter diameter. Test corneal sensation with the Cotton Wisp and the pupillary constriction with the Pen Torch.

3

Clinical Deduction

Correlate physiological changes with Mydriatics (Atropine, Ephedrine, Phenylephrine), Miotics (Physostigmine, Pilocarpine), or Local Anesthetics (Lignocaine).
